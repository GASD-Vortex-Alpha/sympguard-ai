How SympGuard AI checks for emergencies

A deterministic safety check runs on everything you type — separately from, and prioritized over, any AI-generated explanation. It cannot be talked out of flagging an emergency by anything else in the system. It is also not medically exhaustive: it can miss rare presentations or unfamiliar wording, and it can also flag things conservatively that turn out to be less serious. If something feels seriously wrong, trust yourself over the tool.

Emergency numbers are configuration, not hardcoded — see backend/emergency_contacts.json. Numbers shown here are best-effort and may change; verify your local emergency number independently if unsure.

This tool provides general information only and is not a medical diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical advice, and call your local emergency number for urgent symptoms.
