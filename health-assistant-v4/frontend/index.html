Your safety comes first.

SympGuard AI is built so that safety checks can never be quietly overridden by anything else in the system — including AI-generated text.

Emergency red flags are checked by a separate, deterministic system — independent of AI matching.

AI-generated explanations can never override or downgrade an emergency safety result.

Every result is informational — SympGuard AI never states a diagnosis as fact.

Professional medical care is always the recommended path when it may be needed.

About SympGuard AI

SympGuard AI is a symptom-assessment assistant built to help people organize what they're experiencing before talking to a clinician — not to replace one. It combines a curated medical knowledge base, a deterministic safety engine, and an optional AI explanation layer that works even without any AI API key configured.

It was built as a hackathon project. The knowledge base and first-aid content are structured educational scaffolds — see the Evidence section of any result for exactly what is and isn't verified.
